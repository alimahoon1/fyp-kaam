# W1 results: Lahore road network (Ferozpur Road at Ichhra)

Produced by workstream W1 on 15 Nov 2026 in a cloud session (SUMO 1.28.0, libsumo, Python 3.13, torch 2.9.1 CPU). Everything here except `candidates.png` is regenerated by

```bash
python -m manzil.scenarios.osm --validate
```

(about 12 minutes). The network itself is described in `scenarios/lahore/README.md`.

Map data © OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL.

**Status of these numbers:** real SUMO runs on the real OSM road layout, but with **guessed signals, synthetic demand and one seed (0)**. They show that the network and all controllers work on it. They are *not* the FYP-II evaluation (10 seeds, held-out demand, CIs), which is W3's protocol.

## Area choice (`candidates.png`)

Measured on 15 Nov 2026 from OSM API tiles of a strip along Ferozpur Road (lat 31.445–31.535, lon 74.305–74.365; the strip itself was not committed), using `filter_and_clip` and `netconvert_command` from `manzil/scenarios/osm.py` before the cleanup step existed. OSM had **no** `traffic_signals` tags in any of the three boxes.

| Candidate | Hospital | Guessed lights (draft) | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ghazi Chowk | Lahore General Hospital | 10 | Metro at street level (does not match the videos), sparse roads |
| Model Town / Kot Lakhpat | Children's Hospital, Ittefaq | 10 | Elevated metro, mostly one corridor |
| **Ichhra (chosen)** | WAPDA Hospital | 21 | Elevated metro, denser road web (real route alternatives) |

The final Ichhra network has 10 lights: the cleanup removed 12 guessed lights at merge points (two approaches, straight movements only), and removing 5 disconnected one-way fragments dropped the speed sum at one more node below netconvert's guessing threshold. Signal names: S1 Ferozpur Rd × Amrat St, S2 Ferozpur Rd × Fazal Elahi Rd (by the hospital), S3 Kacha Rawa Rd × Shah Kamal Rd, S4 Ferozpur Rd × Wahdat Rd, S5 Wahdat Rd × Faiz Rd / Hussain St, S6 Sultan Ahmed Rd × Malik Fazal Din Rd, S7 Haji Chiragh Din Rd × Rehmanpura, S8 Ferozpur Rd × Canal Bank ramp, S9 Ferozpur Rd × West Canal Bank Rd, S10 Wahdat Rd × Noon Rd (from OSM road names; positions guessed, not surveyed).

## Network checks (`validation.json`)

- `NetworkModel` reads all 10 traffic lights; 0 joined (joined lights are supported and tested on a separate test network).
- Green phases per light: 2 (8 lights), 3 (S4), 4 (S5). Approaches per light: 2–4, so the DQN layout limit (4/4) holds.
- Conflict rule: **0** green phases with a conflicting pair, on every light.
- Headless `sumo` loads the network: yes.
- **All 17 entry roads reach the hospital street** (`routes_to_hospital.csv`: 1.36–2.81 km, free-flow 102–221 s, 1–4 signals on the way). Every entry also reaches every exit (`tests/test_w1_lahore.py`).

## Controller runs (`controller_runs.csv`)

The normal experiment runner with 900 s warm-up + 3,600 s measurement, seed 0, Lahore demand. Waiting time is mean seconds per completed trip.

| Controller | light | medium | heavy | uneven | Safety violations | Collisions |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| fixed-time (netconvert plan, untuned) | 40.6 | 54.1 | 95.8 | 49.9 | 0 | 0 |
| adaptive (grid parameters) | 30.1 | 31.5 | 53.5 | 28.9 | 0 | 0 |
| DQN (grid checkpoint, not retrained) | 150.7 | 316.8 | 399.2 | 307.3 | 0 | 0 |

- **0 safety violations and 0 collisions in all 18 runs** (12 normal + 6 missions), from both the safety filter and the independent safety monitor.
- Fixed-time and adaptive: every vehicle was inserted and there were 0 jam teleports (fixed-time heavy had 1 non-jam teleport).
- **DQN does not transfer**: the grid-trained network leaves traffic waiting about 4–6× longer than fixed-time; at medium/heavy demand 73 and 362 vehicles could not enter and there were 3 and 25 jam teleports. It has to be retrained on this network (request to W3).
- **Max-red finding:** the adaptive controller's longest red was 104 s, above the 90 s `max_red_s`, at S4 (3 phases) and S5 (4 phases) only. The two-phase lights stay at 50 s, as on the grid. Cause: the safety filter's max-red guard forces one starving phase at a time, so with 3–4 phases a second phase can pass the limit while the first is served. This is not counted as a safety violation (no conflicting greens; min green, yellow and all-red kept), but it breaks the max-red bound (request to W4, owner of `manzil/control/safety.py`). Fixed-time max red was 80 s, DQN 90–99 s.
