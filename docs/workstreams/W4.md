# W4 Corridor completion
Status: done (cloud session, branch claude/w4-corridor, 16 Nov 2026)

## Done so far

All numbers are real SUMO runs on the synthetic 3×3 grid, new seeds 10–19, paired against corridor v2. Details, tables and the plot: `results/fyp2/corridor/README.md`.

- **Downstream-capacity check** (D2 §5.6) in `manzil/corridor/coordinator.py`: an exit edge is blocked at ≥ 75 % of its storage with someone halting. Modes are `defer` (≤ 20 s), `flush` (activate the junction at the end of the blocked exit) and `both`. Main study: it never fired (120 runs identical to v2). Spillback stress case (static route, 3 m/s obstruction on `A2B2`): defer +7.0 s (1/10 seeds better), flush +80.5 s (0/10), both +99.8 s (0/10). **Rejected**; kept off by default.
- **Bounded compensation in recovery** (Algorithm 2): after confirmed passage, serve the most-queued denied phase for as long as the junction was held (10–20 s), only after a full 10 s minimum green, then return it to normal control.
  - `comp` (compensate at passage) cut cross-street queueing 6–11 %, but 3 runs were 32–57 s slower for the ambulance, because released traffic queued in front of it.
  - `comp_exit` (compensate only once the ambulance has left its exit edge) had no measurable ambulance cost: median 0 s; mean −0.3, −0.7, +0.2 and +1.2 s with CIs from ±0.5 to ±2.3 s. It cut cross-street queueing by 15.8 % (fixed-time heavy) and 5.4 % (DQN heavy), ≈ 0 at medium demand. Network queue −1.1 / −2.7 vehicles at heavy demand.
  - Stress case: +6.2 s (±19.5), median −0.5 s, but two seeds were +43 s and +65 s.
  - **Kept as the recommended setting; off by default** (see Decisions).
- **Give-way with SUMO's sublane model** (`--lateral-resolution 0.8` + bluelight device, speed mode 31 restored). The ambulance never crossed a red (0 crossings). But SUMO reported **13 collisions in 40 runs** (0 in the 390 runs without the device); the one inspected was a car hitting the ambulance inside junction A0 during its permissive left turn. The sublane model alone raised cross-street queueing by 5–12 %. **Rejected**; kept as an option.
- Safety: 0 safety-monitor violations and 0 ambulance red-light crossings in all 470 runs.
- Bugs found and fixed during the work, before the final runs:
  - A flushed junction beyond the look-ahead could keep corridor ownership without being served.
  - Compensation after a timeout made the ambulance meet reds at junctions it still had to cross.
- Tests: `tests/test_w4_corridor.py` (12 tests).

## Decisions

1. Downstream check: rejected for the grid (never needed; harmful in the stress case). It stays as a tested option, because D2 lists it and Lahore may have real spillback.
2. Compensation: use the `exit_clear` form. Compensation runs only after confirmed passage, never after a cancellation or timeout. Recommended corridor = v2 + `compensation=True, compensation_start="exit_clear"`.
   - **Not made the default yet:** the default must stay v2 so the published Phase 3/5 results, the dashboard and W3's corridor v2 rerun stay reproducible. The switch is a coordinator decision.
3. Sublane give-way: rejected because of collisions and ordinary-traffic cost. Decision 11 (ambulance obeys red) is unaffected.
4. Measurement change: the corridor is now stepped after the ambulance arrives, so recoveries that end after arrival are measured instead of left open. This affects only runs with this code; the Phase 3 numbers were produced before it.
5. Deviation from D2 (to record): D2 §5.6 says "downstream occupancy checks" bound ordinary-traffic harm. On the grid the check never fires and, where it fires, it hurts the ambulance. It is therefore implemented and evaluated but not enabled. The D2 bounds that remain active are maximum priority (60 s) and max-red monitoring.
