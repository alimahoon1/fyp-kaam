## Decisions
- **Classes:** 6 classes, `0 car, 1 bus, 2 truck, 3 motorcycle, 4 rickshaw, 5 van`. Ids 0–3 stay
  the same as the old model, so the old labels and code remain valid. "Rickshaw" covers auto,
  chingchi and loader three-wheelers.
- **Labelling conventions:**
  - pickups with a box or tarp, and the water tanker, are truck;
  - the yellow Mazda minibus is bus;
  - the Suzuki Wagon R is car;
  - the Changan Karvaan is van;
  - a motorcycle box includes the rider.
- **Far field:** vehicles too small to label reliably (above a per-frame row) are not labelled.
  That band is filled grey in train, val and test, so it is neither rewarded nor punished.
- **Split by segment, with a 2 s guard band.** The frames in the guard band, and the title-caption
  frames, are "unused".
- **Bootstrap pre-labeller:** the open-vocabulary detector alone labelled almost every rickshaw as
  car, so a small model trained on the frames already reviewed was used as an extra proposer.
- **Count reference:** the reviewed labels (about 2 fps) are linked into tracks by `GreedyLinker`
  (`manzil/perception/linking.py`). Every frame of the test segments was not labelled, so this is
  the only reference available. Its limit is recorded in the README.
- **Training and evaluation split across kernels:** the weights from training run v1 were saved
  as a private dataset. Evaluation run v2 reuses them (it checks the sha256) instead of training
  again.
- **Deviation from D2:** D2 planned 4 classes. W2 adds rickshaw and van for Lahore traffic. The
  four-class model stays available for UA-DETRAC-style views.

**Report text for Chapter 9** (new section after "Counting and Queue Estimation Results", for
example `\section{Lahore Footage: Rickshaw and Van Classes}`):

> To test the detector on local traffic, two phone videos of Ferozepur Road at Ichhra, Lahore (about 2.3 minutes in total) were split by camera segment into train (134 frames), validation (14) and test (97) sets, with a two-second guard band so that no vehicle appears in two sets. The test set holds the last 30 percent of the first video and the whole of the second video, which was filmed from a different position. The 11,033 boxes were model-assisted, with four detectors proposing boxes, and reviewed frame by frame by the AI assistant; the test labels are preliminary until a team member has checked them. Two classes were added for local traffic: rickshaw (auto, chingchi and loader three-wheelers) and van. The four-class detector was fine-tuned to six classes at an image size of 1280 pixels, with a replay sample of 300 earlier training images. On the Lahore test set the six-class model reached 0.660 mAP50 and 0.566 mAP50-95. On the four original classes it scored 0.711 mAP50, compared with 0.529 for the earlier model. Rickshaws reached 0.743 mAP50, but vans only 0.371, and on the second video only 0.08, so the model does not yet generalise to a new viewpoint for this class. Fine-tuning cost some accuracy on the earlier data (UA-DETRAC mAP50 fell from 0.799 to 0.740, mostly for trucks). In line counting on the three test segments, the new model's count differed from the label-based reference by 2.3 vehicles per segment on average, against 8.7 for the earlier model, which cannot see rickshaws. Full-frame tracking with ByteTrack over-counted motorcycles in dense traffic. These results come from a single location and a few minutes of video and should be read as a feasibility check, not a final accuracy figure.
