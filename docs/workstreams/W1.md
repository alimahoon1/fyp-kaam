## Decisions

1. **Signals are guessed.** OSM has no `traffic_signals` tags in the box (or in either other candidate), so all signals come from netconvert's guessing rule (threshold 40 m/s lane-speed sum). Deviation from D2's "validate TLS programs from OSM": positions and programs are plausible, not surveyed. Must be said whenever Lahore results are shown.
2. **Urban speeds** (60/50/40 km/h) instead of SUMO's rural OSM defaults (80–100 km/h). Also needed technically: netconvert never guesses a signal where any road is faster than 79 km/h, so with the defaults the network had no signals at all.
3. **Left-hand traffic** (`--lefthand`). Without it one netconvert phase had a conflicting pair of left turns.
4. **Cleanup rules** (automatic, recorded in `network.json`): remove guessed lights at merge points (≤2 approaches, straight only); keep only edges on a path from a feeder to a drain of the largest strongly connected part. My first version of the second rule also cut through-carriageways (e.g. southbound Ferozpur Road); fixed before the results were produced.
5. **Traffic lights not joined** by default (`join_tls = False`; close nodes are merged into one junction with `junctions.join-dist 25`). Joined lights had up to 10 approaches and 7 phases, which the DQN layout (max 4/4, `rl_env.py`, W3) cannot take. The reader supports joined lights anyway.
6. **Hospital target** = nearest non-bridge street edge to the WAPDA Hospital node: `25036926` (Fazal Elahi Road). The Ferozpur Road flyover is excluded because the gate is at street level.
7. **Ambulance test origin** = entry whose route to the hospital crosses the most signals (`314653435`, 4 signals, 1.8 km).
8. **Cars only** in Lahore demand, as on the grid (no motorcycles or rickshaws yet).
9.
